## 💫 About Me

I am a **Computer Programming student** at Manisa Celal Bayar University, focused on **XR development** with Unity and C#. Before university I worked as an **IT Support Technician**, which taught me how software is really used and where it breaks. I enjoy turning ideas into interactive 3D experiences, from the model to the code.

## 🔭 Currently Working On

- 🥽 A Unity prototype for **Meta Quest** built around QR-based tracking and measurement
- 🌱 Learning **Machine Learning**, **IoT** and embedded systems alongside XR

## 🛠️ What I Do

- **XR Development:** VR / AR applications in Unity and C#
- **Tracking & Computer Vision:** marker-based tracking and spatial measurement
- **IoT & Embedded Systems:** connected devices, sensors and hardware integration
- **3D Modeling & Design:** assets and parts modeled in Blender and Fusion 360
- **Backend & Data:** C# / .NET services, Python scripts, SQL databases
